Privacy Policy

Eliteworks

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Eliteworks ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information you submit through this website.

Information we collect

When you use our contact, careers or feedback forms, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email address, company, and message. Form submissions are delivered to us through the Web3Forms service. Reviews you choose to share may be stored in a Google Sheet that we control.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, evaluate job applications, and, with your consent, display your feedback on this website. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set tracking cookies of its own.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the information you submitted, or to remove a published review, by contacting us at sales@eliteworks.example.
