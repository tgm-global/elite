Frequently Asked Questions

What does Eliteworks do?

Eliteworks designs, builds, installs and supports industrial automation and robotic systems, including welding, material handling, press line automation, vision inspection, battery assembly and special purpose machines.

Do you provide turnkey solutions?

Yes. We handle the full project: concept, engineering, manufacture, integration, safety, installation, training and after-sales support.

Can you automate our existing production line?

In most cases, yes. We start with a site study of your layout, cycle times and parts, then propose automation that fits around your current equipment.

How do I get a quotation?

Send us your part drawings or samples, current cycle time and target output through the contact page or WhatsApp. We will review them and respond with a proposal.

Do you offer training and after-sales service?

Yes. We train your operators and maintenance staff and provide service, spares and upgrades after commissioning.

Which industries do you serve?

We work with automotive, railway, EV and battery, heavy engineering, agricultural equipment, consumer durables, electrical and general fabrication industries.

Ready to automate your production?

Share your part drawings, target output and current process. Our engineers will review them and reply with a clear proposal.
