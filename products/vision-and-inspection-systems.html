Vision & Inspection Systems

Camera-based inspection and robot guidance that catches defects before they leave your line.

Overview

Visual inspection by people is slow and inconsistent. A machine vision system checks every part, every time, and records the result. It can also guide a robot to the exact position of a part that arrives in a different place each time.

Eliteworks selects cameras, lenses and lighting for your part and surface, develops the inspection logic, and connects the result to your robot, PLC or reject station.

Key Features

  • Presence, absence and orientation checks
  • Dimension and gap measurement
  • Surface and weld-bead inspection
  • Barcode and data-matrix reading for traceability
  • 2D vision-guided robot picking
  • Pass/fail images stored for quality records

Typical Applications

  • Weld seam and spot-weld presence checks
  • Assembly completeness verification
  • Robot guidance for loose or misplaced parts
  • End-of-line final inspection

Benefits

  • Defects found at the source
  • Objective, repeatable decisions
  • Full traceability of every inspected part
  • Less manual inspection effort
